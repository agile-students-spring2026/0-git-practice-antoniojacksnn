# Interesting Article

## Thoughts
What I found interesting is how the article shows Musk tightening the connections between his companies, especially linking AI infrastructure more directly with SpaceX. It suggests he’s thinking long-term about where large-scale computing will live and who controls it. The move feels less flashy than some of his past announcements, but it still hints at a big strategic shift. It makes you wonder how much this is about efficiency versus keeping everything under one roof.
